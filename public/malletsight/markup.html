Chromatic neighbors outside the key. The next note steps back to the scale. Minor leading tones are always written.

Rhythm

Allowed note values. Triplets are placed as a group of three.

Allowed rests

Pushes one note off the beat so it holds across the next pulse. The rest of the bar keeps the rhythms you picked.

Markings

Three slashes through the stem, the usual mallet roll. Long notes are halves and longer. Often includes quarters.

4-mallet blocks

Closed I–IV–V–vi voicings. Left hand takes the bottom two notes, right hand the top two. 1–4 numbers are optional above.
